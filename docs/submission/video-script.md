## Tabs

The match page has six tabs, each with its own link `?tab=`: **Overview** (`overview`, shots 1–4, 6b), **What if** (`whatif`, shot 5), **Replay** (`replay`, shot 6a), **Stats** (`stats`, not used), **Line-ups** (`lineups`, shot 7b), **Preview** (`preview`, shot 7a). The home page without `match=` is the **Match centre** (Live now, Next round, Fixtures, Results, Season so far).

Viewer settings can go in the link: `mode=fan|analyst`, `player=<id>|none`, `team=none`, `lang=en|zh|es`. D. Sahaltessen is `h11`. Pick the team on camera with the **♡** next to the team name in the score band (the top-bar ♥ Team menu works too). **Always put `lang=en` in the link**: without it the home page follows the browser language (it opened in Chinese, "比赛中心", in our profile).

### Which what-if, and why

The video uses the stored what-if **Disallow 17′ goal** at turning point ① (Sadoseno's penalty that put Vantry ahead). In Fan it is the row "What if Z. Sadoseno's 17′ goal hadn't counted?"; in Analyst it is the result shown on arrival. Both read the same stored scenario `S2026-27-TES-VAN:sa3a52146740e` (API: TES win 0.335 → 0.5), so the fan and analyst numbers match (34% → 50%). All 12 changes of this match are stored, so every row of the fan list answers at once. **Do not run a change that is not in the list**: a new scenario takes minutes on the server.

The fan list opens on the biggest change for the picked team (the 54′ goal, 44% to 20%), not the 17′ goal; that is why shot 5 clicks the 17′ row. Once open, the 17′ row's "Why it differs" reads "Disallowing goal boosts Tessmouth United chances" (consistent with the numbers). The 54′ row's "Why it differs" is the template ("Replays: Vantry Town win more likely"); do not linger on it.

### Why this order

Shot 1 shows the new home page and crests, then states the problem. Shot 2 shows the checked explanation on the only model-written, verified card (② and ③ are "Auto summary"). Shots 3–4 are the personalisation rule 4.1 asks for: audience, team and language, then a followed player (timeline, spotlight). Shot 5 contrasts the fan what-if (one plain question and two numbers) with the analyst what-if (effect, intervals, "Clear difference"). Shot 6 shows the pitch with the followed player highlighted and a set piece; shot 7 shows what the page knows before kick-off.
